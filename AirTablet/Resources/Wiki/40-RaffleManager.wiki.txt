# Quick start
- Select or create a venue profile and set ticket price, base jackpot, and winner split.
- Enter the contestant's character name and world, then choose ticket quantity.
- Use VIP / Free for complimentary tickets or BOGO for matching bonus chances.
- Review Tickets and Paid totals before choosing Pick Random Winner.

# Ticket entry
## Tickets
The entered quantity is the purchased or granted base amount.
## VIP / Free
Adds chances without increasing the jackpot.
## BOGO bonus
Doubles the entered chances. By default, the matching bonus does not add to the jackpot. A profile setting can include BOGO bonus tickets in jackpot growth.
## Existing contestants
Adding the same Name and World again increases that contestant's totals. Undo restores the previous raffle state.

# Randomizer
Each ticket is one chance in the weighted draw. A contestant with 20 of 100 tickets has 20 chances out of 100. The winner display shows the selected player, their tickets, jackpot, split, and payout.

# Settings reference
## Profiles
Separates raffle configuration, contestants, branding, and winner history by venue.
## Raffle
Controls ticket price, base jackpot, winner percentage, and BOGO jackpot behavior.
## Branding
Selects the logos used in the randomizer and winner presentation.
## Announcement
Controls whether and where winner text is sent and lets you edit the message template.
## Sound
Controls randomizer tick sound and volume.

# Troubleshooting
## BOGO shows every ticket as paid
Check whether BOGO bonus tickets add to jackpot is enabled for that venue profile. With it off, one purchased ticket produces one paid and one free chance.
## Wrong jackpot or payout
Verify active profile, ticket price, base jackpot, BOGO jackpot setting, and winner split percentage.
## Delete or Clear All appears blocked
Complete the centered confirmation. Tablet confirmations intentionally prevent interaction behind them.
## Logos are missing
Open Branding and verify the local image paths still exist on this computer.
