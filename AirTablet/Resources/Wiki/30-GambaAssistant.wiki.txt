# Quick start: Blackjack
- Select a venue profile and review Rules before starting the night.
- Form the party and use Sync Party Now if the table list is stale.
- Open betting. Record trades or manual deposits, then reserve a bet or pass each player in party order.
- Start Dealing after at least one player has a confirmed bet and no dice request is pending.
- Use Hit, Stand, Double Down, and Split for the active hand.
- Let the dealer resolve, settle the round, and cash players out between hands.

> warning: Keep players in the current party. Party identity is used for dice, trade detection, banks, guided betting, and VIP limits.

# Table controls and round flow
- Open Betting starts the next betting phase.
- Start Dealing closes betting and starts the same initial-deal workflow from the table or overlay.
- Close Betting returns pending wagers without dealing.
- Settle Round resolves active hands and moves to between-hands cash-out.
- Undo Last restores supported dealer actions and removes their queued follow-ups where applicable.
- Panic Stop clears queued chat/dice work and pauses automation for safe recovery.
- Reset Night clears current-night banks, hands, ledger, logs, and history after confirmation.

Broadcast Current Turn repeats the active hand/dealer state. Rebroadcast Banks sends compact bank totals. Broadcast Rules sends the selected profile's current Blackjack limits and key rules to party chat under the heading Profile name Blackjack.

# Betting, banks, and VIPs
Minimum and maximum bet apply to non-VIPs. A profile can store a higher VIP maximum plus Name/Home World entries; it applies only to a matching current party member.

Min Bet, Max Bet, Last Bet, Recent Trade, and Custom Bet reserve available bank gil. Repeated bets add to the active wager but cannot exceed the player's effective maximum. Sit Out / Pass advances guided betting without a wager.

# Trade detection and safety
- Verified incoming gil from a party member adds to their bank; verified outgoing gil subtracts from it.
- Player banks change only after FFXIV reports the gil received or handed over. Ordinary typed chat does not count as a trade.
- Gil direction follows whether FFXIV reports receiving or handing over the gil, not who opened the trade.
- Chat and dice commands pause during a detected trade and resume after Trade complete or a recognized failure/cancel.
- If FFXIV reports that a player is unable to trade, the waiting state clears and the failure appears in Trades.
- A pending player association remains available for a slow response, then becomes stale after two minutes without a gil result.
- Manual Buy-in / Bank Deposit and Manual Cash-Out remain available when automatic detection cannot be used.

> tip: Check Trades and Warnings before adjusting a bank manually so the same transfer is not applied twice.

# Blackjack rules
Cards come from real party-scoped dice results: 1=A, 11=J, 12=Q, 13=K. There are no suits and the deckless model permits duplicate ranks. Initial dealing can use full player hands first or round-robin order.

Profiles control soft 17, ties/pushes, split matching, maximum hands, resplitting, split aces, Double Down conditions, Double Down after split, and whether split 21 is natural. Standard, Double Down, and Natural Blackjack values are total-return multipliers. Rules shows the exact active values.

Broadcast Rules reports active-profile limits, applicable VIP maximum, soft-17/tie behavior, returns, Double Down rule, and split availability.

# Overlay
The overlay follows the tablet theme and supports compact mode plus one or more player-panel columns.

## Context-sensitive controls
- Between hands: Open Betting.
- Guided betting: one eligible player at a time receives Min, Max, Last, Recent Trade, Sit Out / Pass, and a visible Custom bet field with Bet.
- Queue complete: Start Dealing and Close Betting.
- Active player turn: the same valid Hit, Stand, Double Down, and Split actions as Table.
- Settlement: Settle Round.

Irrelevant controls stay hidden. Disabled actions use the same validation and explanations as the Table tab.

## Overlay settings
Configure enable/compact state, panel columns, text scale, render distance, opaque/background style, table-member filtering, drawn overhead mode, and per-player height offsets.

# Profiles, settings, and saved state
Profiles store Blackjack rules, VIPs, and assigned chat templates. They can be created, renamed, exported, imported as a new profile, or removed when safe. Critical profile/rule changes lock during an active session.

General settings cover chat delay, undo depth, automatic trade tracking, optional Astrologian visual actions, Blackjack/DRT autosave, and export location. Chat Templates lists supported variables. Autosave protects active table/bracket state across reloads or crashes.

# Death Roll Tournament
Add entrants from party, target, yourself, manual entry, or timed `!join`. Configure maximum players, channel/delay, opening-zero behavior, next-turn announcements, and real `/dice` requirements for party mode. Monitor the bracket inline or in its separate window; copy the DRT logs when troubleshooting.

# Logs and exports
Players shows banks/status; Dealer Ledger tracks expected dealer gil; Trades shows transfers; History/Export preserves rounds; Log Terminal combines dice, queue, trade, warning, undo, and round-flow diagnostics.

# Troubleshooting
## Start Dealing does not progress
Confirm betting is open, a bet is confirmed, automation is not paused, and no dice is pending. Check each requested roll in Log Terminal; do not repeatedly press Start Dealing.
## A failed trade leaves commands paused
Look for the unable-to-trade toast or system cancel/failure in Trades. If neither was observable, use Panic Stop and verify the bank.
## A completed trade changed no bank
Check automatic tracking, party membership, identity, and the trusted gil plus Trade complete sequence before using a manual adjustment.
## A VIP bet is rejected
Confirm profile, exact Name/Home World, current party membership, and a VIP maximum above standard.
## Dice do not progress a hand
Confirm the dice command and range, expected roller, and pending action. Copy the relevant Log Terminal entries when reporting the problem.
