# Quick start
- Target the prize recipient while they are visible nearby.
- Choose Lock Current Target and verify the displayed Name@World.
- Enter the complete payout amount. PrizeTrader shows how many trades are required.
- Choose Start Payout and verify the recipient and total in the confirmation.
- Keep the recipient nearby while each trade completes.
- Watch Confirmed, Remaining, Current trade, and the progress bar until the payout finishes.

> warning: PrizeTrader is an early-access tool for real gil transfers. Verify the locked recipient, entered amount, every FFXIV trade window, and the final confirmed total before treating a prize as paid.

# Outgoing payouts
FFXIV limits a normal gil trade to 1,000,000 gil. PrizeTrader divides a larger total into trades of up to 1,000,000 gil and continues until the full amount is confirmed. For example, a 2,500,000-gil payout uses two 1,000,000-gil trades and one 500,000-gil trade.

## Locking the recipient
Lock Current Target records the visible player's character name and home world. Starting a payout requires a locked recipient. If the target changes before a payout starts, the displayed progress is cleared so an old amount cannot be mistaken for a new recipient's payout.

PrizeTrader waits when the exact locked player is not visible in the current zone. It does not open a trade with another player who has a similar name.

## Confirmation and progress
The confirmation displays the complete payout and locked recipient before the first trade begins. During the sequence:
- Confirmed is the gil FFXIV has reported as successfully traded.
- Remaining is the unpaid part of the entered total.
- Current trade is the amount assigned to the active trade.
- The status message explains what PrizeTrader is waiting for.

A trade is counted only after the final confirmation and the Trade window closing, with either FFXIV's message reporting the exact gil amount handed over or the operator's gil balance decreasing by exactly that amount. PrizeTrader records the balance just before confirming the trade, then checks every 100 milliseconds until a match is found or three seconds have passed since the final Yes/No confirmation closed. It stops checking the balance immediately on a match and does not wait out the timer before continuing. The main Trade window must still close before the next trade begins. A separate Trade complete message is not required.

Closing, declining, or leaving a trade unanswered without payment evidence does not advance the payout. There is no recipient-response timeout, so the recipient may take the time they need to review and accept each trade. Avoid other gil-changing activities during a payout. Unexpected balance changes stop the sequence for review, and unavailable balances are never treated as zero.

If the recipient completes the trade after the short balance-check window expires, PrizeTrader requires the exact gil handover message. If that message is also missing, verify the payment manually before restarting.

## Failed trades and cancellation
If FFXIV reports a failed trade or the Trade window closes before completion, PrizeTrader pauses without counting that chunk. Check the recipient and current trade amount, then choose Retry Current Trade to repeat the unchanged chunk or Cancel Payout to stop.

Cancel Payout stops future trades but cannot undo gil already confirmed. The status retains the confirmed and total amounts for review. If FFXIV reports gil handed over but completion cannot be verified, check the recipient's balance before starting another payout; do not blindly repeat that payment.

# Incoming trade acceptance
Settings > General includes Automatically accept incoming trades. It is off by default.

When enabled, PrizeTrader can accept an incoming request, wait for the other player to mark their side ready, ready your empty side, and approve the final FFXIV trade confirmation. PrizeTrader does not add gil or items to your side of an incoming trade.

> warning: Automatic incoming trade acceptance applies to any player who sends you a trade request while the setting is enabled. Turn it off whenever you do not actively need it.

Outgoing payout controls are unavailable while an incoming trade is being handled. They return after the incoming trade completes, fails, or closes.

Incoming acceptance works with system messages disabled. PrizeTrader watches the native Trade window and returns to idle when that observed window closes, including after cancellation. Window closure releases the incoming workflow only: it does not confirm a payment, advance an outgoing payout, or change a player bank. A trusted completion message can also release the workflow, without adopting the same closing window again. If an accepted request never opens a Trade window, its pending state clears after ten seconds; an open trade has no recipient-response timeout.

# Control Center widget
Add PrizeTrader's Payout progress widget from Control Center to monitor a payout while viewing another app. It shows a ready, active, retry, incoming-trade, cancelled, or completed state along with the recipient and relevant gil progress.

# Settings and diagnostics
## General
- Automatically accept incoming trades controls the optional incoming-trade workflow.

## Debug Log
The session log records recipient locking, status changes, trade confirmations, failures, and relevant FFXIV trade messages. Choose Copy All for a complete report, select individual text and press Ctrl+C for a smaller excerpt, or choose Clear to start a fresh session log.

The log is kept only for the current session.

# Troubleshooting
## Lock Current Target reports no visible player
Target the nearby player directly and try again. Confirm the target is a player character and their home world has loaded.

## The payout waits for the recipient to be visible
Make sure the locked Name@World is still in the same zone and loaded nearby. Do not change recipients during an active payout; cancel and start again if the intended recipient changed.

## A trade closed or failed
Nothing from that chunk is counted. Review the status and FFXIV messages, then retry the unchanged chunk or cancel. Check the Debug Log before making a manual payment so the same gil is not paid twice.

## The recipient says a trade completed but progress did not advance
Do not assume it was counted. Compare Confirmed and Remaining with FFXIV's trade messages and copy the Debug Log. Cancel if the totals cannot be verified safely.

## Incoming trades are accepted unexpectedly
Open Settings > General and disable Automatically accept incoming trades. The option accepts requests from any player while enabled.
